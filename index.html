惠山泥人

无锡惠山传统彩塑，始于南北朝、盛于明清，列国家级非物质文化遗产。取惠山黑泥捏塑成型、彩绘开相，造型饱满圆润、设色明丽喜庆，传神写照，尤以「大阿福」与戏文人物名动天下。

ENGLISH

Huishan Clay Figurines (惠山泥人), a traditional painted clay sculpture from Wuxi, Jiangsu. Rooted in the Northern and Southern Dynasties and flourishing through the Ming and Qing, it is listed among China's national intangible cultural heritage. Hand-shaped from Mount Huishan's dark clay and vividly painted, the figures are full and rounded, bright and festive — celebrated for the iconic 'Da A Fu' and lively opera characters.
